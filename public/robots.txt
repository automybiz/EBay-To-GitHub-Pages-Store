User-agent: *
# The CMS admin UI is private.
Disallow: /admin

# NOTE: /store and /products are intentionally NOT disallowed here.
#
# The storefront is staged (not launched yet), and its pages carry
# <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">. Blocking them in robots.txt
# would stop crawlers fetching the page, which means they would never see that
# noindex tag — and a URL that can't be crawled but is linked from elsewhere can
# still surface in results as a bare link. Leaving them crawlable is what
# actually keeps them out of search.
