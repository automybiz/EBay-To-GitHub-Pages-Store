Euro Parts West runs on a dual-channel inventory engine designed to give Day the flexibility to sell everywhere while keeping direct profits on his own site:

  1. Channel 1 (eBay Synced Catalog): Automatically mirrors parts listed on the official eBay store. Upscales thumbnails to full 1600px resolution, extracts OEM technical specifications, and strips external links so customers stay on the site.
  2. Channel 2 (Direct Sveltia Inventory): Day’s private dashboard at europartswest.com/admin/ where he can create custom packages, add multiple variants (finishes, sizes, voltages), set direct pricing, and bypass third-party seller fees.

Both streams automatically merge together into the live store catalog at europartswest.com/store/.

Follow these steps to create a custom product with variations:

Step 1: Open the Admin
Navigate to europartswest.com/admin/ and log in using the blue Sign In with GitHub button.
Step 2: Basic Details
Click Create New Entry. Fill in the Title, SKU, Base Price, and Description.
Step 3: Primary Product Image
Drag and drop your hero photo into the Primary Product Image box. This is the photo that shows on the main store catalog grid at europartswest.com/store/.
Step 4: Adding Variants (Sizes, Finishes, Options)
Click + Add Variant at the bottom.
  • Variant Option Name: e.g., Rims 22 Inch Red or 14V / 75 AMP
  • SKU: The specific inventory number for this variant
  • Price & Stock: Independent price and quantity for this option
  • Variant Image: Drop the option-specific photo here. When a buyer clicks this variant pill on the website, the hero photo dynamically swaps to this image!
Step 5: Publish
Click Save in the top right. GitHub automatically triggers a build in the background, publishing the new product live to the website in approximately 45–60 seconds!

The automated script ebay_sync.py handles syncing with eBay:

  • Official API: Authenticates with eBay's developer API using secure credentials.
  • Ultra-High Resolution: Upscales low-res thumbnails to 1600px s-l1600.jpg photos and collects full multi-image galleries (up to 15 photos per part).
  • OEM Specs Extraction: Extracts manufacturer part numbers (MPN), interchange numbers, voltages, and fitment notes directly into structured technical spec tables.
  • Destination: Outputs cleanly to src/data/ebay-products.json. It never touches or overwrites your custom CMS parts!

To retain buyers and eliminate the 13% eBay commission, all buttons route internally:

  • When a buyer clicks Inquire About This Part or Order Direct, they are taken directly to europartswest.com/inquiry.html.
  • Automatic Pre-Fill: The exact part title, brand, quantity, and chosen variant SKU are pre-filled in the inquiry form automatically through URL parameters.
  • CRM Integration: When submitted, the inquiry fires directly into GoHighLevel via webhook, alerting Day immediately via SMS/Email with zero manual entry required.
